06 / AI-ASSISTED ENGINEERING

Faster execution. Same accountability.

AI accelerates execution. Engineering judgment stays mine.

2Set standards
3AI-assisted implementation
4Review
5Test
6Ship

Before AI: I define the requirement and expected behavior, then set the rules, architecture and constraints.

After AI: I review everything it produces, test and validate it, and modify, reject or improve anything that doesn't meet my standards.

I am not learning AI as a specialization and I am not positioning myself as an AI engineer. I use AI as an engineering multiplier to help implement features I have already planned and defined.

08 / HOW I WORK · THE SANDY METHOD

Good engineering is a habit, not a finish line.

01 · Understand

I start with the product behavior and the real problem — not just the endpoint. I ask what should happen, who can do it, and what should never happen.

02 · Build

I turn that into clear backend structure: modules, APIs, data models, access rules and integrations that can evolve without becoming a mystery.

03 · Challenge

I use AI to accelerate implementation, then review the result against my standards. I test, refactor, reject and improve — the final decision stays with me.

04 · Ship

I care about the part after “it works”: production behavior, edge cases, maintainability, security awareness and documentation people can actually use.

For founders, startups and product teams: you don’t just get someone who can write backend code. You get an engineer who thinks about what the system needs to become next.
